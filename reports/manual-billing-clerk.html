1

Introduction — What This System Does

The HWA Smart Metering Portal is a modern, cloud-based tool that connects the water meters installed at customer premises directly to the agency's billing system. It removes most of the manual work of collecting readings, reduces mistakes, and helps customers receive accurate invoices on time.

Here is the journey a single water reading takes — from the physical meter all the way to the customer's invoice:

Smart Meter
records usage
Portal
collects data
Billing Clerk
reviews & approves
Billing System
receives reading
Customer Invoice
generated
Note

The portal does not print or email invoices itself. It only sends approved readings to the billing system. Invoices are created by the downstream billing application using those readings.

Your Role as a Billing Clerk

You are the quality checkpoint in this process. Smart meters are reliable, but a meter can be damaged, misconfigured, or installed at the wrong property. Your daily tasks include:

  • Logging into the portal and reviewing the list of recent meter readings.
  • Checking each reading for correct supply number, reasonable value, and correct date.
  • Posting valid readings to the billing system, one at a time or in a monthly batch.
  • Rejecting faulty readings and notifying the field team or system administrator.
  • Watching for warning messages after posting and knowing what action (if any) to take.
Tip

You do not need to be a computer expert to use this system. If you can browse the web and use simple forms, you can master the portal in a short time. This manual will guide you through every screen.

Logging In

Access to the portal is protected by a username and password. Always sign in from a trusted computer on the HWA network or through the approved remote-access procedure.

  1. Open a web browser Use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox. Type the portal address into the address bar exactly as shown: https://billing.hargeisawatertech.com
  2. Enter your credentials Type your Username and Password into the login form. Passwords are case-sensitive, so check Caps Lock is off.
  3. Click Sign In After a brief loading moment, the Billing Dashboard will appear. This is your home screen.
Security Warning

Never share your password with anyone, and do not write it on paper left near your desk. If you forget your password, contact the IT administrator to reset it. Do not attempt to guess repeatedly — accounts may lock after several failed attempts.

Understanding the Dashboard

The dashboard is designed to give you an instant overview of the latest meter activity. The large table in the center lists the most recent readings received by the portal, newest first.

What Each Column Means

ColumnMeaning
IDThe unique number the portal assigned to this reading. Click it to open the detail page.
Supply NoThe customer account/supply identifier. It must match the account in the billing system.
Reading (m³)Water consumption shown in cubic meters. (See Section 9 for liters vs. cubic meters.)
DateThe date and time the meter reported the reading.
StatusThe current state of the reading: Pending, Posted, Queued/Warning, or Rejected.

Status Badges Explained

Every reading is shown with a colored badge. Learn these colors and you will immediately know what action is needed.

BadgeStatusWhat It MeansAction Needed
Pending Pending The reading arrived from the meter and is waiting for your review. Open and review it. Post if valid, reject if faulty.
Posted Posted The reading has been approved and sent to the billing system successfully. No action. It will be included in the next invoice run.
Queued Queued / Warning The reading was approved, but the push to billing failed temporarily. The system will retry automatically. Do not post again. Wait or inform the system administrator if it persists.
Rejected Rejected The reading was judged invalid and will not be billed. Inform the field team. Cannot be undone from the portal.

4

Reviewing a Single Reading

Before any reading is sent to billing, you should review it. Reviewing is quick but important — it catches problems that would otherwise appear on a customer invoice.

How to Open a Reading

  1. Go to the Readings list Click Readings in the left sidebar. You will see all readings, with the newest at the top.
  2. Use filters if needed If the list is long, enter a Supply No or a Date Range and click Filter to narrow the results.
  3. Click the reading ID Click the blue number in the ID column. The detail page will open.

What to Check on the Detail Page

When the detail page opens, look at the following fields carefully:

  • Supply Number — Does it match a valid customer account? A wrong supply number means the reading may belong to a different property.
  • Reading Value — Is it reasonable for this customer? A domestic customer suddenly showing 10,000 m³ is probably a meter fault.
  • Reading Time — Is the date in the current billing month? Old readings should not be posted unless you are running a special correction.
  • Device EUI — This is the meter's internal serial number. If it does not match the expected meter for this supply, notify the field team.

5

Posting a Single Reading to Billing

Posting means approving a reading and sending it to the agency's billing system. Once posted, the reading becomes part of the customer's billing record.

  1. Open the reading detail page Make sure you have reviewed the reading and that it is correct.
  2. Click the Post Reading button This is the green button at the bottom of the detail page.
  3. Confirm in the dialog A dialog will ask: Post this reading to billing? Click Yes to continue.
  4. System applies the billing date The portal automatically uses the 28th of the billing month as the official billing date. You do not need to enter a date.
  5. Check the result message A colored flash message appears at the top of the page telling you whether the post succeeded.

6

Rejecting a Reading

Rejecting tells the portal that a reading is invalid and should not be used for billing. Use this option carefully; rejected readings cannot be reinstated by a billing clerk.

When to Reject a Reading

  • The value is zero or clearly impossible for the customer.
  • The supply number does not match the meter location.
  • The meter is known to be faulty, disconnected, or replaced.
  • The reading time is far outside the current billing period and appears to be stale data.

Steps to Reject

  1. Open the reading detail page Navigate to the reading you want to reject and click its ID.
  2. Click the Reject button This is the red-outlined button next to Post Reading.
  3. Confirm the rejection A dialog will ask you to confirm. Click Yes only if you are sure.
  4. Check the status The status badge will change to Rejected and the page will show a confirmation message.

7

Bulk Post — Posting All Readings for a Month

At the end of each billing cycle, you can post all remaining pending readings for the month in one operation. This is faster than posting them one by one and ensures the whole batch uses the same official billing date.

When to Use Bulk Post

Use bulk post after the 28th of the month, when most meters have already reported their monthly readings. Do not run it before the 28th unless you have been instructed to do so, because readings received later in the month may be skipped or treated as duplicates.

  1. Go to Readings and click Post to Billing At the top of the Readings list, click the blue Post to Billing button.
  2. Select the billing month Choose the month from the dropdown, for example 2026-07.
  3. Confirm and wait The system will process every pending reading for that month. This may take a minute if there are many readings.
  4. Review the summary message A flash message will tell you how many readings were posted, how many failed, and how many were skipped.

Why Are Readings Skipped?

A reading is skipped during bulk post when it is either a zero reading or already posted. Skipped readings are normal and require no action.

8

The 28th Billing Date Policy

HWA uses a fixed billing date to keep invoices consistent. Every monthly reading is posted to the billing system with the date 28 of that month, regardless of the day the meter actually transmitted the reading.

Company Policy

HWA uses the 28th of each month as the official billing date. This applies to single posts and bulk posts automatically.

How the Date Is Applied

Reading Received OnBilling Date UsedNote
28th of the month2026-07-28Standard monthly reading.
7th of the month2026-07-28Posted early but recorded as the 28th with an automatic description note.
No reading before the 28th—Reading is skipped for that month. No invoice will be generated from this meter.
You Do Not Need to Enter the Date

The portal applies the 28th billing date automatically. You only need to click Post Reading or run the bulk post; the system handles the rest.

9

Reading Values — Liters vs Cubic Meters (m³)

Smart meters transmit water usage as a count of liters. The portal converts that number into cubic meters (m³) because water bills are calculated in m³. One cubic meter equals one thousand liters.

Common Conversions

Liters (L)Cubic Meters (m³)Used In
100,621 L100.621 m³Portal display and billing record
52,789 L52.789 m³Portal display and billing record
1,500 L1.500 m³Portal display and billing record
Billing Server Rounds Down

The billing server receives the value rounded down to the nearest whole cubic meter. For example, a reading of 100.621 m³ is sent to billing as 100 m³. The decimal portion is not lost in the portal, but the downstream billing system uses whole units.

10

Understanding All Status Messages

The portal shows short flash messages after you post, reject, or bulk-process readings. The table below explains every message you are likely to see and what to do about it.

IconMessageMeaningAction
✔ Reading posted to billing. Successfully posted meter reading. All good. The reading is approved and pushed to billing. No action needed.
✔ X reading(s) posted for 2026-07-28, Y skipped. Bulk post succeeded. Some readings may have been skipped because they were zero or already posted. No action needed. Review skipped count if unusually high.
⚠ Reading approved, but the billing push failed and will be retried. The portal accepted the reading, but the billing server could not be reached. Retry is automatic. Wait. Do NOT post again. Inform admin if it remains queued.
⚠ X posted, Y failed (queued for retry), Z skipped. Partial bulk success. Some pushes failed but are queued for retry. Wait for retry. Inform the system administrator if failures persist.
✖ Zero reading; not posted to billing. The meter reported zero usage, which is not valid for billing. Contact the field team to check the meter.
✖ Reading has no supply number. The meter is not linked to a customer account. Ask the system administrator to assign a supply number.
✖ Only pending readings can be posted. The reading is already posted, rejected, or queued. No action. Refresh the page to see the current status.
Keep Calm

Most messages are either success confirmations or routine skips. Only red messages or persistent yellow warnings need follow-up.

11

Common Questions

Below are answers to questions billing clerks often ask. If your question is not listed here, ask your supervisor or the system administrator.

QCan I post the same reading twice?

No. The portal detects duplicates automatically. If a reading has already been posted, any further attempt will be skipped and you will see a message such as Only pending readings can be posted or the reading will be counted as skipped in a bulk run.

QWhat if I accidentally reject a reading?

Contact the system administrator immediately. The portal does not allow clerks to undo a rejection, but an administrator may be able to reverse it from the backend if caught quickly.

QWhy did I get a yellow warning after posting?

The billing server was temporarily unavailable. The portal has queued the reading for retry and will try again automatically. Do not post again. If the warning remains for more than an hour, inform the system administrator.

QThe reading value looks wrong — what should I do?

Reject the reading and notify your supervisor and the field team. Do not post a value you believe is incorrect. Include the supply number and reading ID in your message.

QI posted a reading but no invoice appeared. Why?

Check the portal status first. If it shows Posted, wait a few minutes for the billing system to process it. If it still shows Pending or Queued, contact the system administrator.

QWhat does already posted for this supply mean?

This is normal if you run bulk post more than once for the same month. The duplicate reading is counted as skipped. No action is needed — the customer will not be double-billed.
