What to check before posting:

  • The Reading Value is reasonable (not zero, not abnormally high).
  • The Reading Time is within the current billing month.

5. Posting a Single Reading to Billing

  1. Open the reading detail page Navigate to Readings → click the reading ID.
  2. Click "Post Reading" A confirmation dialog appears: "Post this reading to billing?"
  3. Click OK to confirm The system sends the reading to the billing server using the 28th of the reading's month as the billing date (see Section 7).
  4. Check the result message A green or yellow banner appears at the top of the page.

6. Bulk Post — Posting All Readings for a Month

At the end of each billing cycle, use the Post to Billing bulk action to post all approved readings for a given month at once.

  1. Go to Readings → Bulk Actions Look for the "Post to Billing" button or form at the top of the Readings list.
  2. Select the billing month Choose the year and month (e.g., 2026-07). The system will use the 28th of that month as the billing date.
  3. Click "Post to Billing" The system processes each meter's latest reading on or before the 28th.
  4. Review the summary message You will see how many readings were posted, failed, or skipped.

What "skipped" means

  • The reading is zero — zero readings are never sent to billing.
  • The reading was already posted for this supply in a previous run.

7. The 28th Billing Date Policy

Company Policy HWA uses the 28th of each month as the official billing date for all meter readings. This applies whether you post a single reading or run the bulk post.

What this means in practice:

SituationWhat the system does
Reading was taken on the 28th Posted with date 2026-07-28. No note added.
Reading was taken on a different day (e.g. the 7th) Posted with date 2026-07-28. A description note is added: "No reading on 2026-07-28; used last available reading from 2026-07-07."
No reading exists on or before the 28th The reading is skipped for that month's bulk post.
You do not need to do anything special. The 28th billing date is applied automatically — you just click "Post Reading" or "Post to Billing" as usual.

9. Reading Values — Liters vs Cubic Meters

Smart meters transmit their readings in liters internally. The portal automatically converts and displays all values in cubic meters (m³) for your convenience.

The billing server also receives the value in cubic meters (rounded down to a whole number). For example, 100.621 m³ is sent to billing as 100 m³. This is correct and matches HWA's billing formula.

10. Common Questions

Can I post the same reading twice?

No. The system checks for duplicates automatically. If a reading for a supply number has already been posted this month, it will be skipped and you will see a "skipped" count in the bulk post summary.

What happens if I accidentally reject a reading?

Once rejected, the reading cannot be re-posted from the portal. Contact your system administrator to reverse the status in the database.

Why does the system show a yellow warning after I post?

The reading was approved and saved, but the billing server was temporarily unavailable. The system retries automatically. You do not need to post again — doing so will create a duplicate.

What does "already posted for this supply" mean?

The billing server already has a reading for this meter and month. This is normal if you run the bulk post more than once in the same month. The reading is counted as "skipped" — no action needed.

The reading value looks very different from what the field team reported. What should I do?

Click Reject on that reading and note the reason. Then notify your supervisor and the field team to verify the meter on-site. Do not post a reading you believe is incorrect.

I posted a reading but no invoice appeared in the billing system. What do I do?

Check the reading's status in the portal. If it shows Posted (green), the data has been sent — the invoice should appear in the billing system within a few minutes. If it still shows Pending or shows a warning, contact your system administrator.
